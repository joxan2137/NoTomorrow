No Tomorrow v2

A working prototype. Everything on the phone responds, with invented sample data.

  1. Today Tap I'm in, then Start workout.
  2. Workout Tick sets. The first working set beats your bench record. The rest timer starts on its own.
  3. Minimize the workout with the chevron and walk around the tabs.
  4. Finish and watch Today, Train, Progress and Bro update.
  5. Fuel Try AI photo, Barcode and Search. Tap the ring.
  6. Progress Switch lifts, ranges, and Body.
  7. Bro Send a heads-up, or say you can't make it.

Reload to start over. On a phone, open this page directly for a full-screen version.
