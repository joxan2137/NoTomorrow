Track bodyweight and timed exercises, see which muscles an exercise works, get told when a new version is out, and use redesigned Android widgets.

## What's new

### Exercises
- **Bodyweight and timed exercises (iPhone).** Push-ups log reps without a weight, and planks log seconds held. Change how an exercise is tracked with **Track as** in its **…** menu during a workout. Sets you already ticked keep their numbers.
- **A new exercise sheet.** Tap an exercise to see a looping 3D demo, a 3D body with the muscles it works lit up, and form cues in English and Polish.
- **Pick exercises by muscle.** The body-map button in the exercise picker filters the list to one muscle.

### Update reminder (iPhone)
- When a newer release is out on GitHub, a banner at the top of the app says so. Tap it to open the release, or dismiss it until the next one.

### Android widgets
- Quick log, Fuel calendar, Gym week and Break timer have a new look: a lit dark surface, macro bars, calendar stats, a weekly progress bar and a glow when your rest is over.

## Good to know

- Everything from 0.6.1 carries over, including the machine scanner. Your data is kept when you install over it.
- The 3D demos in this build are the first version. The redone demos come in a later release.

## Downloads

- **Android:** `NoTomorrow-0.6.2-android.apk`. Sideload it on Android 8.0+ (enable "install unknown apps"). It installs over 0.6.1 and keeps your data. The `.aab` is for Play Console uploads.
- **iOS:** `NoTomorrow-0.6.2-ios-unsigned.ipa`. It's unsigned: install it with AltStore, Sideloadly or similar, which re-sign it with your Apple ID. HealthKit sync needs a paid developer certificate, so it isn't available in re-signed builds. Requires iOS 17+.

Binaries are built by the [release workflow](https://github.com/joxan2137/NoTomorrow/blob/main/.github/workflows/release.yml) from this tag.
