# No Tomorrow 0.4.0 — calmer screens, clearer focus

The v2 redesign (see `design/v2/`) on iOS and Android. Same five tabs, same look; each screen now has one
focal card, data is drawn where a shape says more than a number, and your partner shows up where it matters.

**Today**
- The week strip shows who trained: an ember dot for you and, when paired, green for your partner (rose when they
  missed). Gym days still ahead get a thin ring.
- The next-session card is the screen's focal card.
- The Fuel row's ring is split by protein, carbs and fat, with the macro bars in the same colours.

**Train**
- An "Up next" card on top: the routine Today suggests, its exercises with sets × reps, and Start (Resume while a
  workout runs). The other routines get a small play button.
- History is grouped into This week, Last week and Earlier.

**Workout**
- A thin rail under the header shows how far along each exercise is; tap a segment to jump to it.
- Suggested weight: when every set of the last session hit its target at one weight, the exercise suggests the next
  step (+2.5 kg / +5 lb). "Use" fills it into the open sets; nothing changes without the tap.
- Bigger rounded ticks, and done sets get a faint ember tint instead of being dimmed.

**Fuel**
- The kcal ring shows what the calories came from (protein blue, carbs amber, fat violet); the macro bars and the
  "protein to go" hint use the same colours.

**Progress**
- Lifts opens on a chart: pick a lift, see its estimated 1RM, the change over 1M / 3M / 1Y / All, and the trend.
- "Muscles this week": a body map coloured by sets per muscle, the five most-trained muscles, and which big ones
  haven't been trained yet. The body-weight card now lives on the Body tab only.

**Bro**
- The shared week is the screen's focal card.
