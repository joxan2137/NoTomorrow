Bolder Android widgets: fewer things on each, and each one bigger.

## What's new

### Android widgets
- **Quick log.** The small widget is now just the calorie ring, as big as it fits, and one button for your usual food. The wide widget adds protein, carbs and fat against your goals, with as many quick-log buttons as fit under them. It shows real numbers even before you've logged anything.
- **Fuel calendar.** It's now just the calendar: one row per week up to this one, each day a coloured tile with its date, a dot on days you trained and a ring around today. Make it taller to see more weeks. The side stats and legend are gone.
- **Gym week.** The day of your next session is the heading, then the routine, then the time, in much bigger type. The wide widget shows the whole week across its width.
- **Break timer.** Bigger rings and numbers, taller buttons, and a bold "Rest is over. Go." when your break ends.

## Good to know

- Everything from 0.6.2 carries over. Your data is kept when you install over it.
- Nothing changes on iPhone in this release.

## Downloads

- **Android:** `NoTomorrow-0.6.3-android.apk`. Sideload it on Android 8.0+ (enable "install unknown apps"). It installs over 0.6.2 and keeps your data. The `.aab` is for Play Console uploads.
- **iOS:** `NoTomorrow-0.6.3-ios-unsigned.ipa`. It's unsigned: install it with AltStore, Sideloadly or similar, which re-sign it with your Apple ID. HealthKit sync needs a paid developer certificate, so it isn't available in re-signed builds. Requires iOS 17+.

Binaries are built by the [release workflow](https://github.com/joxan2137/NoTomorrow/blob/main/.github/workflows/release.yml) from this tag.
