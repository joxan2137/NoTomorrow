Your past quick adds and AI meals now show up in search too.

## What's fixed

### Fuel
- **Past meals are in your foods.** On first launch, every quick add and AI-scan meal already in your diary is saved to your foods, so it shows up under Recent and in search. Each name is saved once, with the figures from the last time you logged it.
- **Save on an edited meal saves the food.** Tapping a quick add or AI meal in your diary and pressing Save now keeps it in your foods as well, with your changes.

## Good to know

- Everything from 0.6.6 carries over. Your data is kept when you install over it.
- The fix is on iOS and Android.

## Downloads

- **Android:** `NoTomorrow-0.6.7-android.apk`. Sideload it on Android 8.0+ (enable "install unknown apps"). It installs over 0.6.6 and keeps your data. The `.aab` is for Play Console uploads.
- **iOS:** `NoTomorrow-0.6.7-ios-unsigned.ipa`. It's unsigned: install it with AltStore, Sideloadly or similar, which re-sign it with your Apple ID. HealthKit sync needs a paid developer certificate, so it isn't available in re-signed builds. Requires iOS 17+.

Binaries are built by the [release workflow](https://github.com/joxan2137/NoTomorrow/blob/main/.github/workflows/release.yml) from this tag.
