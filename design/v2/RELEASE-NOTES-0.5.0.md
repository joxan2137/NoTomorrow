Home-screen widgets on iPhone and Android, and a new sound when your rest is over.

## What's new

- **Quick log widget.** Shows how many kcal you have left, plus the foods you log most. Tap one to log it for today without opening the app.
- **Fuel calendar widget.** Your Fuel history heat grid, with a dot on every day you trained. The large size adds the 7-day and 30-day averages and your sessions in the last 30 days.
- **Gym week widget.** Your next session and this week's gym days, with your partner's days when you're paired. On iPhone it also works on the Lock Screen.
- **Break timer widget.** Tap 1:00, your default rest or 2:00 to start a break without opening the app, then +15 or Skip while it counts down. It's the same rest timer the workout uses, so the Lock Screen countdown and the notification come with it.
- **Rest-over sound.** A short chime now plays when a rest ends, with the notification and inside the app during a full-screen workout. It follows the ringer switch and doesn't stop your music.

## Good to know

- **iPhone (sideloaded):** open the app once after installing so the widgets can fill in. If a widget keeps saying "Open No Tomorrow once to fill this widget", your sideloading tool didn't keep the app's shared data permission.
- **iPhone:** a food logged from the widget shows up in the app's Fuel screen the next time you open the app.
- **Android:** the "Rest is over" notification category was replaced to carry the new sound, so any custom settings you gave it are reset.

## Downloads

- **Android:** `NoTomorrow-0.5.0-android.apk`. Sideload it on Android 8.0+ (enable "install unknown apps"). It installs over 0.4.1 and keeps your data. The `.aab` is for Play Console uploads.
- **iOS:** `NoTomorrow-0.5.0-ios-unsigned.ipa`. It's unsigned: install it with AltStore, Sideloadly or similar, which re-sign it with your Apple ID. HealthKit sync needs a paid developer certificate, so it isn't available in re-signed builds. Requires iOS 17+.

Binaries are built by the [release workflow](https://github.com/joxan2137/NoTomorrow/blob/main/.github/workflows/release.yml) from this tag.
