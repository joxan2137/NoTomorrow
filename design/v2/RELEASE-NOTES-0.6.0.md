Build your own routines, plus the tools other gym apps have: plates, warm-ups, supersets, records, imports from Strong and Hevy, and more.

## What's new

### Train
- **Routines.** Tap **+ New routine** next to Routines on the Train tab to build one. Name it, add exercises, and set sets, reps and rest for each. Tap a routine to edit or rename it. The **…** button on each routine, or a long press, has Edit, Duplicate, Share, Move up / down and Delete. Any past workout can also become a routine with **Save as routine**.
- **Programs.** **Browse programs** on the Train tab adds a ready-made plan as routines: Full body 3× a week, 5×5 strength, Push / Pull / Legs or Upper / Lower.
- **Share a routine as text.** Send a routine to a friend; they paste it into **Import routine** to get their own copy.

### During a workout
- **Plate calculator.** Shows which plates go on each side of the bar for the weight you entered.
- **Warm-up sets.** One tap adds warm-up sets that ramp up to your working weight.
- **Notes and RPE.** Add a note to any exercise and an RPE to any set.
- **Supersets.** Link exercises so they run back to back.
- **Rest per exercise.** Each exercise can keep its own rest time.
- **Replace or move an exercise** without losing the sets you've logged.
- **Exercise history.** See what you lifted last time without leaving the workout.
- **Share card.** A picture of the finished workout to post or send.

### Exercises
- **Favorites** sit at the top of the exercise picker, and **equipment filters** narrow the list.
- **Custom exercises** can now be edited and deleted.

### Progress
- A **training calendar** with every day you trained, and **weekly stats** from Monday to Sunday.
- **All-time records** and **lifetime milestones**, which also show on the finish screen.
- On a lift's page: a **rep-max table**, **1RM percentages** and a **1RM calculator**.
- **Body measurements** and **progress photos** on Progress > Body. Photos stay on your phone and are kept out of iCloud and computer backups.

### Import
- **Bring your history over** from Strong, Hevy or a No Tomorrow CSV export in Settings > Import workouts.

## Good to know

- Everything from 0.5.0 carries over. Your workouts, routines and settings are kept when you install over it.
- Screen reader labels were reviewed on all the new workout and progress screens.

## Downloads

- **Android:** `NoTomorrow-0.6.0-android.apk`. Sideload it on Android 8.0+ (enable "install unknown apps"). It installs over 0.5.0 and keeps your data. The `.aab` is for Play Console uploads.
- **iOS:** `NoTomorrow-0.6.0-ios-unsigned.ipa`. It's unsigned: install it with AltStore, Sideloadly or similar, which re-sign it with your Apple ID. HealthKit sync needs a paid developer certificate, so it isn't available in re-signed builds. Requires iOS 17+.

Binaries are built by the [release workflow](https://github.com/joxan2137/NoTomorrow/blob/main/.github/workflows/release.yml) from this tag.
